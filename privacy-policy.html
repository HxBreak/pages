Policy Statement

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Declaration

Effective Date: August 11, 2026 Application: DevCheck Developer: HxBreak
Solemn Commitment to Absolute Local Execution Non-Negotiable

DevCheck is architected upon a strict, uncompromising premise: all data remains exclusively on your physical device. The application does not collect, record, transmit, share, or sell any personal information or device telemetry. No cloud infrastructure is utilized, and no remote analytical, tracking, or advertising modules exist within the software.

I. Foundational Architecture & Zero-Collection Guarantee

Under the Google Play Developer Policy and standard international data privacy principles, data collection is formally defined as the transmission of information from a user's terminal to an external environment.

DevCheck performs all computational operations, inspections, and formatting purely in volatile local memory. The application possesses no communication channels to convey data externally. Specifically:

  • No Network Permissions: The application does not declare or request the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission. It is structurally precluded by the operating system from executing outbound network calls.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: There are zero integrated telemetry SDKs, crash report collectors (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry), or audience measurement frameworks.
  • No Identity Association: No user accounts, registration tokens, or persistent device fingerprints are gathered, synthesized, or maintained.

III. Permissions & Runtime Consent Protocol

DevCheck adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege:

  • Normal Permissions: The application declares ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE solely to ascertain the active connectivity type on the device. This does not allow network communications or access to private personal data.
  • Sensitive Runtime Permission (Location): In compliance with the Android security framework, reading precise Wi-Fi access point identifiers (SSID and BSSID) is gated behind ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION. DevCheck never requests this permission during installation or launch. It is queried solely when the user intentionally navigates to the Wi-Fi inspection interface.
  • Pre-Prompt Prominent Disclosure: Prior to invoking any platform permission dialog, an explicit explanation is presented detailing the purpose of the inquiry.
  • Autonomous Consent Management: Users retain unconditional authority to grant, deny, or revoke access at any time via Settings → Data Access within the application or via system settings. Denying permission leaves the remainder of the diagnostic suite entirely functional.

IV. Google Play Data Safety Compliance Disclosures

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety declaration mandates, DevCheck certifies the following responses within the Play Console:

  • Does the app collect or share any user data? — No. Per official guidelines, on-device data access that is never transmitted off the user's terminal constitutes local processing, not collection.
  • Is data encrypted in transit? — Not Applicable (no data is transmitted off the terminal).
  • Does the app provide a mechanism for users to request data deletion? — Not Applicable (no records exist remotely). Deletion of the app from the terminal erases all localized configurations.
  • Third-Party Sharing: Zero user or device data is shared with any third party, government entity, or advertising syndicate.

V. Data Retention & Local Storage

DevCheck maintains no remote storage infrastructure or external databases. On the local device, the software persists exclusively:

  1. The user's explicit consent state preferences regarding optional diagnostic modules.
  2. Theme and aesthetic display preferences.

All stored state is sandboxed within private application storage. Uninstalling the application completely purges all associated records through standard operating system cleanup.

VI. Children’s Privacy

DevCheck does not target, market to, or knowingly solicit interaction from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable statutory threshold in relevant jurisdictions). As a zero-collection tool, no personal data from minors or any other individuals is ever accepted or preserved.

VII. Amendments & Notifications

Should any structural amendment to this policy occur, the updated document will be published immediately to this canonical location with a revised effective date. Given that the software maintains no external communication channels, material changes regarding permission handling will be communicated via in-app presentation upon upgrade.
